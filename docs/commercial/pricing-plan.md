# Pink Elephant Limited — Pricing & Monetization Plan

## Guiding principle

Our apps are **free for consumers, forever**. We believe on-device AI is a private
utility, and we never monetize by selling user data, showing ads inside the tools, or
charging for basic features. Instead, revenue comes from what the products enable:
businesses and developers who want the same private-on-device experience at scale.

## Consumer pricing

| Tier | Price | What's included |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | HK$ 0 / US$ 0 | The entire product line — all 9 apps, all features, offline, no ads, no telemetry. |

The flagship **Pink Elephant Talk** is fully functional for free: chat, thinking
mode, web search, document reading and photo vision — no paywall, no credits.

## Revenue streams (non-consumer)

### 1. White-label & OEM licensing
We license **Pink Elephant Talk** and the utility series to device makers,
telecom operators and brands who want a private, on-device assistant pre-loaded on
their hardware — with their own branding, colors and system prompts. Pricing is per
unit-per-device or annual flat license.

### 2. Enterprise / B2B deployments
- **On-device assistant for regulated industries** (banking, healthcare, legal) that
  cannot send data to public clouds. We package the app with a client-selected
  open-source model and provide deployment, tuning and support.
- **Custom datasets & fine-tuning** — we train small, private fine-tuned models for
  clients and deliver them as GGUF, ready to run on-device.
- **API/embed SDK** — a software development kit that embeds on-device chat into a
  client's own Android/iOS app.

### 3. Distribution partnerships
Play Store listing, carrier app stores and OEM bundles bring users to the free apps;
high-volume distribution is the entry point for white-label conversations.

### 4. Optional premium services (future)
If ever introduced, premiums would be **privacy-preserving** conveniences (e.g.
priority model updates, additional voice packs) — never a gating of core features,
never ads, never data sales.

## Why free works for us

- **Trust is the product.** A privacy-first company that charges nothing for its
  core value builds the reputation that enterprise partners pay for.
- **Proven on-device tech.** Pink Elephant Talk already ships a working
  vision-language model in an APK — a rare, demonstrable capability.
- **No marginal cost.** On-device inference has no per-query API cost, so "free" is
  economically honest.
