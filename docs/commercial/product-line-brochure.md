# Pink Elephant Product Line — Brochure

**Pink Elephant Limited** · Hong Kong · Privacy-first, on-device, free.

Nine Android apps. Zero cloud AI. Zero telemetry. Every product below is free,
works offline and asks for no sensitive permissions.

---

## 1. Pink Elephant Talk — v7.2 (Flagship)

**Your private AI assistant — chat, photos, documents and our own Cantonese voice, all on your phone.**

The assistant lives on your device. Type a question and it answers; show it a
photo and it describes what it sees; attach a document and it reads it; and it
reads its answer aloud in **粵語 Cantonese, 普通话, English or Auto**. There is
no cloud, no account and no API fee — your conversations never leave the phone.

**Features**

- **On-device assistant** — a vision-language model runs entirely on your phone,
  ~1.5 GB one-time download, cached on device.
- **Native Cantonese voice (粵語)** — a genuinely on-device Cantonese voice is
  bundled in the app. No system Mandarin fallback; real spoken Cantonese, offline.
- **Voice-language selector** — the header 🌐 / 🗣️ control switches between
  **Auto / 粵語 / 普通话 / English**, remembered between sessions.
- **Photo vision** — attach a photo; the app sees and describes it.
- **Reliable live answers** — weather, news, prices and sports are fetched live
  (Open-Meteo, Google News RSS, Wikipedia) with sources cited.
- **Document reader** — attach `.txt`, `.pdf` or `.docx`; text is extracted
  on-device and the app answers questions about it.
- **Multilingual** — answers in the language you write in.
- **Private by design** — no account, no telemetry, no camera/microphone
  permissions, no cloud inference.

*See `data-sheet.md` for the full technical specification.*

## Our promise

- **Free** — the entire product line is free of charge.
- **Offline** — every app works without a connection (Talk needs internet once to
  download its model, and optionally for live facts).
- **Private** — no accounts, no telemetry, no selling of data.
- **No permissions abused** — the utilities request zero permissions; Talk requests
  only internet (model download + live facts), uses Android's system pickers for
  documents and photos, and its voice synthesis runs entirely on-device.
