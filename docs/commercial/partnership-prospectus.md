# Pink Elephant Limited — Partnership & Distribution Prospectus

**A private, on-device AI assistant you can brand as your own.**

## The opportunity

Most AI assistants today require accounts, cloud APIs and sending user data to a
third party. Enterprises in banking, healthcare, legal and the public sector — and
device makers serving them — increasingly cannot accept that. Pink Elephant Limited
offers what they need: **a genuinely on-device, privacy-first assistant** that ships
as a small APK and runs a real vision-language model on the phone itself.

## What we bring to a partnership

1. **Working on-device product.** Pink Elephant Talk v7.2 is already built,
   installed and verified on hardware — an APK that runs fully offline with a
   real vision-language assistant on the phone.
2. **Capabilities that matter.** Text chat, photo vision, document reading,
   a native bundled **Cantonese voice (粵語)**, a voice-language selector
   (Auto / 粵語 / 普通话 / English), and reliable live web facts
   (weather/news/prices/sports) with cited sources — all in one compact package
   with zero telemetry.
3. **Compliance-ready.** Open-source base model with attribution retained; clean
   licensing; no data-collection surface.
4. **A complete product line.** Nine free apps that make attractive bundles for
   carriers and device makers.

## Partnership models

### A. White-label & OEM
We rebrand Pink Elephant Talk (and optionally the utility apps) for your devices —
your logo, your colors, your system-prompt persona — and you ship it pre-loaded or
in your store. We handle the build, signing and updates.

**Good for:** Android phone/tablet OEMs, telecom operators, retail brands.

### B. Enterprise on-device AI
We deploy the on-device assistant inside your organization: client-selected model,
tuned system prompt, managed distribution, support. No user data ever leaves the
devices.

**Good for:** banks, insurers, hospitals, law firms, government agencies, schools.

### C. Custom fine-tuning
We train small, private fine-tuned models on your data and deliver them as GGUF,
ready to run on-device — giving you a specialized assistant at zero per-query cost.

**Good for:** domain-specific assistants (support, training, field operations).

### D. Embed SDK
We license an embeddable SDK so your own app can host on-device chat inside your
existing user experience.

**Good for:** software vendors adding a private assistant to their product.

## What a partner needs to know

- **No cloud dependency** — inference happens on-device; there is no per-query cost
  and no data egress.
- **Model licensing** — the default base model is open-source (Apache-2.0), so
  redistribution is clean; other models can be substituted per engagement.
- **Security** — no accounts, no telemetry, no sensitive permissions; content is
  processed locally and deleted with the app.
- **Roadmap** — faster GPU backends, more voice languages and additional document
  formats are in active development.
