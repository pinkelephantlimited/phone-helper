# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Pink Elephant Talk runs fully on-device: your conversations, photos and
documents never leave your device and are not uploaded to any server.

If you discover a security issue, please report it privately — do not open a
public issue. Contact: Pink Elephant Limited, Hong Kong — via
[our Hugging Face organization](https://huggingface.co/pinkelephantlimited).

We will acknowledge reports as soon as practical and keep you informed as we
investigate. We do not pay bounties at this time.

## Scope

- The Android/iOS app source in this repository
- The VLM training pipeline (`train/`, `data/pipeline/`)
- HF model weights and GGUF builds we publish

## Response

We aim to respond to valid reports within a few business days and work with
you on a coordinated disclosure.
